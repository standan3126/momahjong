🎋 Sanctuary Guide

🀄 How to Play
Tap two identical free tiles to clear them. Clear every tile to win!
A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and its left or right side is open. Blocked tiles look dimmed.
Coloured tile edges show the layer: jade → teal → gold → crimson → violet (higher). Dots in the corner count the storeys.
Quick matches play a little tune. There's no clock: take your time.
⭐ Stars, Gold & Rank

Every level gives up to three stars: ⭐ for finishing, ⭐⭐ with at most one hint or undo, ⭐⭐⭐ with no power-ups at all. You also earn gold (more for more stars, double on bosses, half on replays).

Your stars raise your Sanctuary Rank. Higher ranks sometimes grant a free Divine Blessing match.

🌱 Novice: 0 ⭐
🥈 Silver: 25 ⭐ • 5%

🟡 Sage: 150 ⭐ • 12%
👑 Imperial Master: 300 ⭐ • 16% Blessing
🎁 Power-Ups
Your own are used first. When you have none, the badge shows a price and tapping buys one with gold.
📜 Undo (10 ): Brings your last matched pair back.
💡 Hint (15 ): Highlights a free matching pair.
🌪️ Shuffle (25 ): Rearranges the tiles so there are moves again. Free when you're completely stuck.
🔥 Dragon Torch (40 ): Burns away a matching pair, even a buried one.
🦅 Eagle Eye (40 ): Unmasks every Mystery tile for the rest of the level.
✨ Special Tiles & Levels
🌟 Golden tiles: +5 gold when you match them.
🎁 Treasure chest: every 5th level, one spin for gold or power-ups.
🔮 Mystery tiles: Hidden until they become free. Tap to peek.
👹 Boss boards come every 10 levels from 20. Each opens with 16 ⭐ from the 9 levels before it; replay a level or two from the 🗺️ map if you need more. Some levels have a special modifier.
